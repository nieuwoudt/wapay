Thanks for the update, and no problem on the compliance timing.

Yes, we apply a risk-based, tiered KYC model, in line with FICA's risk-based
approach:

1. Every client is identified at onboarding. The account is bound to their
   cellphone number, verified by OTP on WhatsApp, before any transaction is
   possible. One number, one account.

2. Simply buying a product (for example an OTT voucher) requires nothing
   further, the same as buying one at a till. Wallet activity is deliberately
   low-value and low-risk: deposits and payment requests are capped at R3,000
   per transaction, and the wallet is spend-only today. There is no cash-out
   anywhere in the live product.

3. Full identity verification (SA ID verified against Home Affairs data, with
   a liveness selfie match through an accredited verification provider)
   becomes mandatory the moment a client wants money OUT. No payout or
   withdrawal will ever run without it. The payout rail is not yet live to
   customers; it launches with this verification enforced from day one. This
   is also why I asked for OTT's KYC requirements in my earlier email: we want
   our verification thresholds to line up with what your compliance team
   needs before we switch payouts on.

Happy to walk your compliance team through the onboarding and verification
flow in detail if that would help.
