Subject: Collect — one question that decides it for us

Hi Keamo

Coming back to you on Collect as promised.

Having gone through the agreement carefully, Collect as drafted doesn't fit
WaPay. It's built around a physical footprint: a terminal base of vendors,
cash floats at each point of sale, branding erected at premises, and a
Consumer who walks up to a counter. Clause 16.3 puts it beyond doubt — "the
Consumer may only be paid out in cash and in South African Currency."

WaPay is digital-only. We have no branches, no vendors, no cash. We couldn't
perform that contract on day one, so signing it wouldn't be honest.

But there's a version of this we'd sign immediately.

Our customers hold WaPay wallets on WhatsApp. If someone receives a Standard
Bank Instant Money, Nedbank or VodaPay voucher, the valuable thing for them
is not cash in hand — it's the value landing straight in their WaPay wallet,
in seconds, without travelling to a till. Same redemption on your platform,
same voucher, same validation; only the settlement leg differs: instead of a
vendor handing over banknotes, we credit the Consumer's wallet with the full
face value.

Your own clause 9.6 is already a pure system-to-system handshake — your
platform validates the voucher and notifies our system. And clause 16.16
contemplates the Instant Money option being presented on materials "digital
or physical". So the platform doesn't appear to be the obstacle; the cash
wording is.

So, three questions:

1. Do you have a digital variant of Collect, where a wallet operator settles
   the Consumer by crediting their wallet instead of paying cash? If that
   paper exists, please send it and we'll review it straight away.

2. If not, would OTT consider a variation permitting wallet-credit
   settlement for a digital Representative?

3. Most important: is the "cash only" requirement OTT's own, or does it come
   down from Standard Bank, Nedbank and Vodacom as a scheme rule? If it's a
   bank requirement, a variation between us doesn't solve it and we'd need
   their consent — worth knowing before either of us spends time on it.

To be direct about why this matters to us: this would be one of the most
valuable things we could offer our customers, and it would send meaningful
redemption volume your way. Cash-in for unbanked customers is the hardest
problem we have, and Instant Money is how millions of South Africans already
move money. We're not asking for a discount — we're asking whether the
product can settle digitally.

If the answer is no, no hard feelings at all. We'll focus on Payout and
Redemption and leave Collect there.
