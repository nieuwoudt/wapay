1. SERVICES AND ACTIVITIES PROVIDED AND ACCEPTED

WaPay is a South African, WhatsApp-based consumer wallet. In relation to
the OTT voucher, WaPay:

  - Sells OTT vouchers to its users through the WaPay platform (as an OTT
    reseller, at face value — any WaPay service fee is itemised separately
    from the voucher value, never a markup on the voucher).

  - Lets users send or gift vouchers to one another as a store of value —
    for example, buying a voucher to send to a student or a family member —
    the same way airtime is gifted.

  - Accepts the voucher as a payment method on the WaPay platform, where the
    holder redeems its value for airtime, data, prepaid electricity, fuel
    (through our UniFuel integration on the YoYo application) and other
    products and services offered on WaPay, including at selected retailers.

  - Recipients can also spend the voucher across OTT's accepting merchant
    network, under the standard OTT voucher terms.

In short, the voucher is used exactly as OTT defines it: sold, gifted, and
redeemed as a payment method on accepting platforms — WaPay being one — and
across OTT's merchant network. It is a goods-and-services voucher on our
side, not a cash instrument, and is never exchanged for cash.

On the money-in side, customers load their own funds only through PayFast,
our regulated South African payment provider (card and EFT) — for example,
to buy a voucher or airtime on WaPay — so deposits ride licensed payment
rails and WaPay never handles card or bank-account details itself.

On withdrawals, for completeness: where a WaPay user has loaded their own
funds (by card or EFT) and wishes to move that balance to their South African
bank account, this is handled through OTT's Payout product (PayShap / RTC),
under the Payout agreement already in place with you — in other words,
through a service OTT itself provides. Two points compliance will want to be
clear on: (a) this is a bank payout of the customer's OWN loaded funds, and
(b) it is kept entirely separate from the voucher. The OTT voucher is not
cashable and is never converted into a withdrawable balance; voucher value is
only ever redeemed for goods and services. So the payout rail and the voucher
sit in two separate lanes and never cross.

2. CROSS-BORDER MOVEMENT OF FUNDS

None. WaPay operates solely within South Africa: users onboard with South
African cellphone numbers, every transaction is denominated in Rand, and all
settlement is to South African bank accounts. There is no cross-border
movement of funds of any kind.

3. LOCAL WEBSITE ADDRESS

wapay.co.za (the service itself runs through WhatsApp, with payment pages on
pleasepayme.co.za).
