WaPay chat landing page: mock, version 2

Drawn from the founder's annotated screenshot of 24 Nov 2026 and his answers the same day. One screen for everyone. The layout is the pattern he pointed at (mark, greeting, message box, pill menu); the colour, type and mark are WaPay's own. Nothing here is built. The amounts are examples.

Decided 24 Nov: the page follows the phone's dark or light setting · a member is greeted "Good morning, first name" · the balance shows under the greeting with a hide button · the one-tap chips stay · the menu is Chat, Pay, Activity, Profile · a visitor's message box reads "Your cell number" · the microphone and + stay.

  • Menu: Chat, Pay, Activity, Profile. Links moves under Pay so the pill holds four items like the screenshot. For a visitor the last three are greyed and say "Open your account first" when tapped.
  • Top left: help, language, log in or log out. Top right (members): hide or show the balance.
  • Dark or light: decided: the page follows the phone's own setting. Both are drawn above; the light one is today's app look.
  • Sign-up on this screen needs three decisions: how the cell number is proven (recommended: one tap that sends a code from the person's own WhatsApp), whether a first PIN may be chosen on the web, and the terms line. They are in section 6.9 of the plan.
  • Microphone: the phone keyboard's own dictation at first. Recorded voice to a model is a later decision.
