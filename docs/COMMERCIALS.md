*Written 2026-10-04 by the "WaPay Money Map" thread from docs/HANDOVER_COMMERCIALS_V1.md. Every number below is either cited to a file, a contract, an email or a function in the code, or marked ASSUMED. The dashboard built from the same data is the WaPay Money Map artifact (URL in docs/HANDOVER_COMMERCIALS_V1.md section 1 and WAPAY_STATUS.md). Nothing in this document changes a fee in code; every fee change is a founder decision and appears in section 5 as a question.*

## 1. How to read this document

**Five statuses, one per number.** A row has up to three numbers: what the counterparty charges us (cost), what the customer pays WaPay (price), and what the supplier pays us (commission). Each carries its own status:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SIGNED | In an executed contract, or on a supplier rate card WaPay is actually charged against. |
| LIVE | In code and charged to customers today, on a rail that is on in production. |
| ASSUMED | Used in code or in a guide without a contract or a recorded founder decision. |
| PROPOSED | A recommendation, a supplier offer, or a recorded decision not yet charged (rail off) or not yet agreed by the counterparty. |
| UNKNOWN | Asked of the supplier or never asked; no answer on file. |

Product state (live, pilot, built, rail off, not built) is shown separately from the status of the numbers: a live rail can carry an assumed price, and a signed price can sit on a rail that is off.

**VAT.** WaPay is not VAT-registered (`WAPAY_VAT_REGISTERED` is the switch for registration day). Every supplier price quoted excluding VAT costs WaPay 1.15 times the quoted figure, and that VAT cannot be recovered. WaPay charges no VAT on its own fees. Every cost line below shows both numbers.

**Who pays.** "Customer, on top" means the fee is added to the amount (deposits). "Receiver, off the credit" means the fee comes out of what the person getting paid receives (pay links); the payer always pays exactly the displayed amount, because charging more for card is prohibited in South Africa (PayFast T&C clause 5.3, SARB, PASA, the schemes). "Nobody" means face value or free.

**Reference amounts.** R50, R200, R500, R1,000 and R3,000. Margin is what WaPay keeps on one transaction after the counterparty's cost including VAT; a negative margin is money WaPay pays to run the transaction. "n/a" means the amount is outside the row's limits.

**Where code and a document disagree,** the code is what is charged today and the document is the proposal, and the disagreement is a question in section 5.

**Sources.** Code: `lib/ledger-core.js` (FEES, cashoutFeeCents, cashoutRailCostCents), `lib/deposits.js` (depositFeeCents, paymentRequestFeeCents, cardRailFee), `lib/fee-facts.js`, `lib/payouts.js`. Contracts: the OTT Payout Agreement (signed 2026-08-25) and its 2026 Addendum, the OTT Reseller Agreement (countersigned 13 August 2026), the OTT Voucher Merchant Agreement (WaPay signed about 4 September 2026, countersignature not on file), the BLD Voucher Redemption Agreement 2025, the Pay@ Pay Out schedule (November 2025), the PayFast portal rate card (2026-09-04), the SHB/Adumo offer (2026-09-10), the Yoyo email (September 2026) and Yoyo's Agreed Gift Card Rebates sheet. ## 2. What the map says (the findings)

1. **Real money moves on one rail, and the deposit fee is sized for its worst method.** The deposit fee is one number for every PayFast method (4.20% + R2.30, rounded up to the next rand) while PayFast's cost differs by method. At R200 the margin is R6.40 on Instant EFT, R1.34 on a credit card, R0.65 on a debit card, and R3.95 lost on Mukuru Cash. Mukuru Cash is switched on in the PayFast portal and loses money on every deposit under about R2,850; SCode and MoreTyme are correctly off. The 4.20% versus 4.50% decision has been open since 2026-09-04.

2. **Both voucher load rails lose 90 cents per R100, by contract.** The BLD Voucher Redemption Agreement (Annexure A clause 2.1) and the OTT Voucher Merchant Agreement (Annexure A) both charge 6% of face value **excluding VAT**. The ledger credits face less 6% (R94.00 on R100), so the issuer remits R93.10 and WaPay credits R94.00. At R500 the gap is R4.50, at R3,000 it is R27, plus a week of float on the OTT side (weekly settlement in arrears). The locked NET credit policy was meant to make loads never loss-making; the VAT makes them loss-making by 0.9%.

3. **OTT pays 4% on every voucher WaPay issues, and the ledger books none of it.** The Reseller Agreement is executed (OTT countersigned 13 August 2026, effective 11 August 2026, 4% on reconciled sales, monthly reconciliation into the prepaid wallet). `FEES.voucherGift.commissionBps` is still 0 "until signed in writing", so `REVENUE:COMMISSION:VOUCHER` is zero and every voucher's margin is understated by R4 per R100. A gift earns R3 plus 4%; a self-purchase earns 4%.

4. **Pay-outs are margin-positive on every method at every amount, and dearer than every bank.** PayShap keeps a constant R5.12 (R8 fee, R2.88 cost), RTC R4.82, cash R4.81 to R8.19 across the bands, Pay@ R4.05. Against the market: PayShap R8 versus R0 to R2 at the banks; cash R18 versus Absa R16, FNB R11, Capitec R10, Shoprite R9.99. The +R2 on every method (2026-09-11) is the founder's open question; the payouts thread's docs/PAYOUT_COMMERCIALS.md carries the recommendation. FNB eWallet is unpriced by OTT and assumed equal to CashSend; RTC has no provider on the sandbox; R12.69 of sandbox float deductions on 2026-10-04 do not yet reconcile to the addendum rates, so every applied pay-out cost is unverified until OTT's first statement.

5. **Pay links: the subsidy is real and bounded; Adumo only pays on debit.** Under R50 is free by decision (2026-08-27) and the taper to about R56 is loss-making by design: a R50 request paid by credit card costs WaPay R3.14. Above the taper the PayFast rail earns R1.04 (credit) to R6.10 (Instant EFT) at R200 and R15.60 to R59.30 at R3,000; a debit card at R200 earns 35 cents. The Adumo rail at R1 + 2.5% (rail off, SHB offer unaccepted) earns R1.74 on a R200 debit card and loses on every pure credit-card ticket (R0.56 at R200, R9.45 at R3,000).

6. **Vending commissions are planning estimates.** Airtime 4%, data 5% and electricity 1.5% appear in no Blu document anywhere in the workspace (the Blu thread searched every folder on 2026-10-04); Blu's own product sheet says Eskom electricity commission is per unit, not a percentage. Fuel wiCodes at today's R0 fee lose 2.5% plus R0.20 per voucher before a R15,000 monthly retainer; with the 5% convenience fee discussed, break-even is about 1,220 vouchers of R500 a month. Retail wiCodes have no rebate agreed anywhere (Yoyo: none on grocery or fuel, KFC possibly), while the code would book 3%. The four categories asked of Blu (lifestyle, bill pay, gaming, remittance) are UNKNOWN end to end.

7. **Sending is free and costless; business is R0 a month on the same rails.** WaPay-to-WaPay sends and balance-paid links are internal ledger moves with no rail cost. The R2.50 cash-balance send fee exists in code with no customer path. Business pricing has no subscription or per-link fee on file; the B2B disbursement price (R8 per successful pay-out to the business) is a 2026-08-10 sketch against a market where Ozow publishes R3.

8. **Costs that are not per transaction.** The Yoyo retainer (R15,000 a month, proposed); WhatsApp messages billed inside the 24-hour window from 1 October 2026 (utility rate, about R0.13 a message after 1,000 free a month); pay-out reversals R10 ex VAT. None is recovered by a fee today.

The chat quotes every fee from the functions that charge it (`lib/fee-facts.js`, `lib/how-it-works.js`, generated into docs/CONVERSATION_KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md): 4.2% + R2.30 on deposits, 6% kept on a Blu voucher, R3 on a voucher gift, free WaPay-to-WaPay, free under R50 on pay links, R8 / R10 / R18-R23-R30 on withdrawals, no fee on airtime, data and electricity, no monthly fee. The Feature Catalogue of 2026-09-28 matches the code. The customer Pricing Guide PDF of 2026-08-19 (marketing/guides) does not: it prints the pre-decision pay-out fees (R6 / R8 / R12 / R14), "EFT and Capitec Pay load free" and "R6 per R100" on OTT loads. That is question 28 in the sign-off list.
