WaPay Money Map

Every component of money in, money out, vending and getting paid: the counterparty, what it costs WaPay with the VAT it cannot recover, what the customer pays and who pays it, what is left at R50, R200, R500, R1,000 and R3,000, and whether each number is signed, live, assumed, proposed or unknown.

WaPay is not VAT-registered: supplier ex-VAT prices cost 1.15 times No card surcharging: the payer always pays the displayed amount Flat customer fees, never percentages Text master: docs/COMMERCIALS.md

Counts are of individual numbers (a cost, a price or a commission), not of rows. The fee arithmetic on this page is a mirror of lib/ledger-core.js and lib/deposits.js, held equal by tests/commercials-consistency.test.mjs.

Needs the founder's signature

Every assumed, proposed or unknown number, as one question each

A number without a contract or a recorded decision is a guess the product is charging or absorbing today. Each question names the row it belongs to and who can answer it. Nothing here changes a fee in code; the answer does.

    How to read a status

    Where the code and a document disagree, the code is what is charged today and the document is the proposal; the disagreement is one of the questions above. Product state (live, pilot, built, off, not built) is shown separately from the status of each number, because a live rail can carry an assumed price and a signed price can sit on a rail that is off.
